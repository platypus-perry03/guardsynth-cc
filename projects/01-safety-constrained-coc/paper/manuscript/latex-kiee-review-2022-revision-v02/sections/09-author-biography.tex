\textbf{김진현 (Jin Hyun Kim)}\par
\smallskip
\raggedright
Jin Hyun Kim received the B.S. degree from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies, Republic of Korea, in 1999, and the M.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University, Republic of Korea, in 2001 and 2011, respectively. He is currently an Associate Professor in the Department of AI Information Engineering at Gyeongsang National University, Republic of Korea, where he has been a faculty member since 2019. He was a postdoctoral researcher at the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), Daejeon, Republic of Korea, from 2011 to 2012, and at Aalborg University, Aalborg, Denmark, from 2012 to 2014. He was a researcher at INRIA/IRISA, Rennes, France, from 2014 to 2015, and a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Pennsylvania, USA, from 2015 to 2019. His research interests include cyber-physical systems, real-time systems, model checking, formal methods, and the safety and trustworthiness of medical and autonomous AI systems.
