Seven conditions with supplied rules at evaluation

P0 + rule reuses a CoC-only trained P0 checkpoint and supplies the exact frozen P1 natural-language rule at evaluation. Its ZS-CoT and basic PS variants add the same native prompting/extraction procedures used in the completed P1 controls. P1 and its variants use rule-trained P1 checkpoints; P2 uses its own CNL-trained checkpoint and semantically identical rules.

Three existing checkpoint seeds (42, 17, 123), 480 shared test rows per condition/seed: 240 temporal, 144 static, 96 maneuver. Three newly evaluated P0 conditions yield 4,320 additional predictions. Four previous conditions are reused unchanged. Together the table covers 10,080 predictions, not 10,080 independent road scenes. No retraining or human labeling was performed.
